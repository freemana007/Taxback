What can I claim?

Answer yes or no. Every “yes” is money you can probably get back.

    How to claim your refund

    Revenue has two websites for claiming. Pick the one that fits you.

      Let’s find your refund

      Most people who work for an employer are owed something back. You don’t need to know anything about tax. It takes about 10 minutes.

          How do you want to enter your pay?

          Read a payslip

          Pick a PDF payslip and the app finds the pay and tax on it. It's read on this device only; the file isn't saved or sent anywhere.

          Pay for each month

          Copy “Taxable pay” (or “Gross pay”), “PAYE” (income tax), “USC” and “PRSI” from each payslip. Only pay and income tax are needed. Paid weekly? Type each week with + between, like 812 + 812 + 812 + 812.

          Check your USC and PRSI

          The app works these out from your pay. Add what you paid to see if it was right.

          Benefits from work: are they taxed?

          Is your tax right this year?

          Check the credits Revenue gave your employer, spot emergency tax, and test each payslip. Most people who overpay find the cause here.

          Reviewing tax year .

          Tax Credit Certificate

          In myAccount: PAYE Services → View tax credit certificate → pick the year. For each job, copy the yearly tax credits, the yearly rate band (also called the cut-off point) and the basis.

          Payslip check

          Works out the income tax each payslip should take, using your certificate, and compares it with what was taken. Needs your pay entered month by month under Income.

          Taxed together or separately?

          Check Revenue’s Statement of Liability

          After you claim, Revenue sends a Statement of Liability. Enter its figures (or read the PDF) to compare with this app.

          What this does not cover

          • PRSI for the self-employed (Class S) and the USC surcharge on self-employed income over €100,000. Employee PRSI is worked out on your average week, so it can differ by a few euro from your payslips.
          • Self-assessed (Form 11) income, rental income, Rent-a-Room relief, Help to Buy.
          • Separate or single assessment of married couples, widowed or single-parent credits.
          • Dependent relative credit and other less common credits.

          This is an estimate to help you organise your claims. It isn't tax advice; check anything sizeable with Revenue or an accountant.
